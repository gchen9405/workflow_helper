Order fulfillment at our warehouse:

A new customer order comes in through the web shop. First the system checks
whether every item is in stock. If everything is in stock, the warehouse team
picks and packs the order and hands it to the carrier for shipping; once the
carrier confirms delivery, the order is complete. If something is out of
stock, purchasing places a backorder with the supplier and the order waits
until the missing items arrive, after which it goes back to the stock check.
