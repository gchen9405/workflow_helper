Problem: reviewing new papers for our team's reading list is chaotic. People
post links everywhere and we never decide what actually gets discussed.

What I'd like: each week we have a short, agreed list of papers to discuss,
and nothing worth reading gets lost.

My rough idea so far: someone suggests a paper in a shared channel; once a
week the team lead looks through the suggestions and picks the ones for the
meeting. I'm not sure what should happen to the ones that aren't picked, and
whether we need some kind of vote.
